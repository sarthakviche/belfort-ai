What we do

Clarity before capital.

Most tools hand you indicators and leave the conclusion to you. Belfort AI reduces each stock to a decision frame you can inspect, question and test.

01

One label, one rule

Setup valid, Watch, or Avoid, produced by deterministic code. The same numbers always give the same label, and every unmet condition is listed.

02

Risk before reward

The main risk in plain language, the invalidation price, and a position size rounded down so a stopped-out loss never exceeds the limit you chose.

03

Every figure sourced

Each number carries its source and date. Anything that could not be fetched is shown as missing, with the reason, rather than guessed.

The dossier

Everything behind the label.

Six sections open from every report, so the verdict is never a black box.

  • Why this label. The rule, one condition at a time.
  • Levels and patterns. Support, resistance, Fibonacci, detected shapes.
  • Business. Returns, debt, growth and sector peers.
  • What would make me wrong. Concerns ranked by severity.
  • Pattern reliability. How often each pattern actually worked.
  • Sources. Where every number came from, and when.

Belfort AI

Belfort AI is an educational research project. It is not investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security, and its author is not a SEBI-registered research analyst or investment adviser. Labels describe the state of a setup under fixed rules; they do not predict returns. You can lose money. Prices are end-of-day, not live.
